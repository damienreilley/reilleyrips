Sources and set facts

Manufacturer final checklist: 55 base rows and 34 autograph rows (89 entries). Kruz Schoolcraft is included in both. Degree Grading omits him from the base list; the final manufacturer checklist takes precedence. Team labels are as printed, not current roster claims.

Base Series: 30 packs × 5 cards, advertised 5 on-card autographs. Mega Series: 10 packs × 10 cards, advertised 10 on-card autographs. Rare Diamond Avengers and inscriptions are advertised, but a full verified checklist or print run is not available here.
